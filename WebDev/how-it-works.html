Common Questions
Do I need a credit card to sign up? +
No. The free plan is free forever — no card required. You only upgrade when you're ready.
What payment methods can my clients use? +
Your clients can pay via Paystack, Flutterwave, or direct bank transfer. All major Nigerian banks supported.
Can I customise invoices with my logo? +
Yes. Upload your logo, set your brand colors, and choose from multiple professional templates.
How do I send invoices via WhatsApp? +
After creating an invoice, tap "Send via WhatsApp". It opens a pre-filled message with your invoice link ready to send.
